## PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Article Eater** is a software system that ingests scientific papers about how architectural environments affect human cognition, emotion, health, and behavior. The system evaluates the quality and plausibility of research findings using a structured "web of belief" informed by mechanistic knowledge from neuroscience, cognitive science, and environmental psychology.

**Opus's role**: Theory architect. Opus convenes expert panels to build the mechanistic knowledge that populates the system's tiers of belief. The panels produce documents containing expert position statements, cross-examination debates, formalized mechanistic templates, testable predictions, and full reference lists. These documents are then encoded into the software by other AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity) per a task distribution plan.

**The three-tier belief structure**:
- **Tier 1**: Foundational neuroscience mechanisms (predictive processing, neural pathways, reward circuitry, etc.) — high confidence, cross-domain
- **Tier 2**: Mid-level architectural theories (ART, SRT, Biophilia, Prospect-Refuge) — reduced to Tier 1 mechanisms
- **Tier 3**: Specific empirical findings from papers — evaluated against Tiers 1 and 2

## KEY CROSS-DOMAIN FINDINGS

### 1. vmPFC as Domain-General Subjective Value Node
Confirmed across SIX domains: architecture (aesthetic preference), education (schema congruence), music-pitch (prediction value), music-rhythm (groove value), music-emotion (aesthetic emotion integration), aural architecture (cross-modal aesthetic value). Same computation: integrate prediction outcomes + reward signals + memory associations + self-relevant meaning → subjective value.

### 2. Goldilocks Zone / Inverted-U for Prediction Error
Confirmed across FIVE domains: visual complexity (D ≈ 1.3), musical information content (moderate IC), rhythmic syncopation (moderate), harmonic surprise (moderate), spatial scale (moderate exceeds schema but remains assimilable). Moderate PE → optimal aesthetic/emotional response. Extended: awe occupies HIGHER PE range (still assimilable but requiring schema revision).

### 3. Architecture IS Composition
Both are the deliberate orchestration of prediction error over time/space for emotional effect. Music: temporal sequence of auditory PE. Architecture: spatial sequence of multi-modal PE (visual + auditory + haptic + proprioceptive). Same Goldilocks principle, same vmPFC convergence, same multiplicative mechanism convergence at peak moments.

### 4. BRECVEMA Mechanisms Are Sound-to-Emotion (Not Just Music-to-Emotion)
7 of 8 BRECVEMA mechanisms apply to architectural acoustics. Musical expectancy (M) extends to acoustic spatial expectancy. Architectural spaces are "acoustic emotional instruments" with characteristic BRECVEMA-A activation profiles.

### 5. PE Continuum: Pleasure → Awe → Terror
Not a simple inverted-U but a complex curve with multiple peaks. Aesthetic pleasure at moderate PE (reward circuit: NAc, vmPFC). Awe at high PE (DMN reconfiguration, lateral PFC, small-self). Terror at extreme PE (amygdala, stress response). Great architecture navigates this continuum deliberately.
