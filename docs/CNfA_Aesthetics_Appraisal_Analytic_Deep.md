
# CNfA Aesthetics Appraisal Framework
## Analytic + Predictive Coding + Stress-Test Edition

Version: 1.0 (Deep Theoretical Expansion)

---

# PART I — FORMAL ELIMINABILITY ARGUMENT

## 1. The Question

Is "beauty" a necessary explanatory primitive in architectural cognition?

We define:

B = Beauty predicate  
M = Set of mechanistic appraisal variables  
R = Observable responses (ratings, RT, physiology, approach)

---

## 2. Eliminability Thesis

A theoretical term T is eliminable if:

1. All predictive work can be performed without T.
2. T does not introduce intervention-stable causal leverage.
3. T does not support additional invariant generalizations.

---

## 3. Formal Structure

Premise 1:
R can be predicted by M.

Premise 2:
B is inferred from R.

Premise 3:
Interventions target M, not B.

Premise 4:
No evidence shows B has independent causal force.

Conclusion:
B is theoretically eliminable in explanatory models of architectural response.

---

## 4. Objections and Replies

### Objection 1: Neural commonality (mOFC activation)

Reply:
Valuation circuitry is domain-general.  
It supports reward computation, not a unique beauty module.

---

### Objection 2: Phenomenology feels unified

Reply:
Unified feeling does not entail a unified mechanism.  
Vector aggregation can feel singular.

---

### Objection 3: Beauty has normative force

Reply:
Normativity belongs to cultural theory, not mechanistic cognition.

---

# PART II — PREDICTIVE CODING INTEGRATION

## 1. Generative Model Sketch

Let x be image features.

Brain has generative model p(x | θ).

Prediction error:
ε = x − E[x | θ]

Free energy approximation:

F ≈ ε² + model complexity penalty

Pleasure may correlate with efficient reduction of ε.

Moderate ε → learning + interest  
High ε → threat  
Low ε → boredom

---

## 2. Mapping to Appraisal Hubs

Prediction error → PredictabilitySurprise  
Variance in ε across scales → ComplexityRegulation  
Unexpected high-frequency spikes → ThreatSafety

# PART III — STRESS TESTING THE ONTOLOGY

We test the framework against architectural edge cases.

---

## Case 1: Brutalism

Features:
- High angularity
- High massiveness
- Low ornament

Possible Appraisals:
- ↑ ThreatSafety (ambiguous)
- ↑ SocialSignaling (institutional authority)
- Moderate ComplexityRegulation

Outcome:
Some find awe; others find hostility.

Ontology handles variability via moderators.

---

## Case 2: Kitsch

Features:
- High ornament
- Predictable symbolism
- Bright color saturation

Appraisals:
- High Predictability
- High SocialSignaling (pop taste)
- Possibly low Fluency if cluttered

Explains polarized reactions.

---

## Case 3: Sacred Gothic Cathedral

Features:
- Extreme verticality
- Repetition
- Light shafts
- Fractal tracery

Appraisals:
- High Prospect
- High SocialSignaling
- Moderate Complexity
- Moderate Surprise
- Low Threat

Outcome:
Awe without invoking beauty primitive.

---

## Case 4: Minimalist Gallery

Features:
- Low entropy
- High symmetry
- High coherence

Appraisals:
- High Fluency
- Low Complexity
- High SocialSignaling (elite minimalism)

Outcome depends on task goal (work vs contemplation).

---

## Case 5: Chaotic Market

Features:
- High clutter
- High entropy
- Organic structure

Appraisals:
- High Complexity
- High Surprise
- Moderate AffordanceFit
- Variable Threat

Explains engagement + fatigue.

---

# PART IV — LIMIT CONDITIONS

When might the ontology fail?

1. If a stable latent variable predicts responses independent of all hubs.
2. If interventions on hubs do not alter reported beauty.
3. If a domain-specific neural circuit for beauty is discovered.

Until then, parsimonious mechanistic explanation preferred.

---

# PART V — SYNTHESIS

Architectural environments modulate:

- Efficiency systems
- Prediction systems
- Safety systems
- Control systems
- Restoration systems
- Social inference systems

The interaction of these produces affective states.

Language compresses that into "beautiful."

---

# FINAL POSITION

Beauty is a summary term.

Mechanisms are explanatory.

Interventions should target appraisal hubs, not abstract aesthetic labels.
