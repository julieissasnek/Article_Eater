## WHAT THE PROJECT IS

Article Eater is a software system that helps experts evaluate scientific papers about architecture and human wellbeing. It uses a **web of belief** — a structured knowledge graph where higher-tier beliefs (theories, mechanisms) inform how lower-tier beliefs (individual findings) are evaluated.

**Three-tier structure:**
- **Tier 1**: Mechanistic templates (T1–T52, M1–M17, AX1–AX6, L1–L5, MAT1–MAT5, SC1–SC4, VF1–VF3, plus TP-series in progress) — formalized causal pathways
- **Tier 2**: Domain theories (ART, SRT, Biophilia) — reduced to Tier 1 mechanisms via formal ReductionClaim objects
- **Tier 3**: Individual findings from papers — evaluated against Tier 1/2 knowledge

**Unifying theoretical framework**: Predictive processing / prediction error (PE). The brain continuously generates predictions; architectural properties confirm or violate those predictions; the magnitude, valence, and resolution of PE determines cognitive and emotional response. vmPFC is the domain-general value computation node.

## MAJOR THEORETICAL DISCOVERIES THIS SESSION

### 1. Dual-Pathway Architecture Is a General Principle
- **Light**: image-forming (rods/cones → V1) vs. non-visual (ipRGCs → SCN)
- **Touch**: discriminative (Aβ → S1) vs. affective (CT → posterior insula)
- Same physical stimulus, two neurally distinct systems, different cortical targets, different functions. Architecture can optimize for one at expense of the other. Best experience engages BOTH simultaneously.

### 2. Multi-Modal Convergence Is a General Principle
- **L3** (daylight): 5+ channels (circadian, visual, attentional, temporal, view)
- **MAT4** (wood): 5 channels (visual-fractal, haptic-thermal, acoustic, olfactory, ecological)
- **VF2** (visual nourishment): multi-scale visual engagement
- Each channel contributes modest effect; convergent total is larger, potentially super-additive. Structural parallel is deliberate — how multisensory perception works.

### 3. Space Is the Scaffold
- Spatial configuration (SC1–SC4) is PRIOR to sensory templates — you must be in a space before perceiving its light, materials, and sound
- SC3 (promenade) is the TEMPORAL ORGANIZER — determines when all other templates activate during movement
- Three nested scales: micro (isovist), meso (promenade), macro (cognitive map)

### 4. The Goldilocks Model Has a Beyond-Goldilocks Region
- Eisenman case (SC-I, VF-I): architecture that deliberately exceeds the Goldilocks zone produces intellectual engagement / sublime discomfort rather than distress, IF the occupant has agency and the PE serves a purpose
- Three beyond-Goldilocks regimes: (a) awe (AX3, vastness + accommodation), (b) sublime discomfort (Eisenman, deliberate challenge), (c) bad design (T14, no agency, no purpose)
- Agency + purpose modifier determines valence of high PE
