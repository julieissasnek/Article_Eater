# Criteria of Excellence: Exemplar Panel Reports

Based on a programmatic and qualitative audit of all 49 historical panel documents, we have identified the core structural elements that produce the highest-fidelity gap closures and mechanism calibrations. 

Future LLM-driven expert panels must adhere to the following **Criteria of Excellence** to be considered successful outputs.

## 1. Early Ideation & Perspective Sharing (The "Round Table" Phase)
The most successful panels (e.g., Panel 30, Panel 51) are characterized by an extremely diverse array of voices participating immediately in the document.
* **Criterion:** Before open debate begins, every panelist must formally introduce their ideas, sharing their distinct theoretical perspective and the empirical justification for it.
* **Metric:** A high density of distinct speaker introductions within the first 25% of the transcript.

## 2. Heated and Far-Reaching Dialogue (The "Crucible" Phase)
True consensus requires friction. The strongest templates emerge from panels that actively dispute weak evidence and push back on broad generalizations.
* **Criterion:** Discussions must not be a flat sequence of agreements. Panelists must actively disagree, contend alternative views, challenge assumptions, and introduce critical caveats.
* **Metric:** Frequent usage of debate discourse markers (e.g., "I must push back," "however, I contend," "an alternative view is," "I challenge that assumption"). Panel 55 (Creative Cognition) remains the gold standard here, displaying intense conceptual friction before synthesizing a final parameter.

## 3. Substantial Depth and Duration
Robust mechanism extraction requires sustained, exhaustive elaboration. Rapid convergence usually indicates shallow reasoning.
* **Criterion:** The mechanism chains must be modeled with immense specificity down to the circuit or neurotransmitter level, requiring sustained, multi-turn dialogue.
* **Metric:** Target length >10,000 words. Quick summaries are insufficient.

The following documents should be referenced as the gold standard for formatting, pacing, and rigor:

### 1. `30_Panel_MIII_Musical_Emotion_Mechanisms_V1_0.md`
* **Strength:** Masterclass in **Early Ideation**. The panel achieved extreme diversity of voice immediately, with dozens of distinct perspectives laid out in the opening fraction of the document, ensuring no single framework dominated the baseline.

### 2. `55_Panel_CREA_I_Creative_Cognition_V1_0.md`
* **Strength:** Masterclass in **Heated Dialogue**. This document contains the highest density of debate markers in the entire repository. The panelists actively fought over the DMN-ECN coupling mechanisms, resulting in an incredibly rigorous, battle-tested final output.

### 3. `51_Panel_MATII_Materials_Calibration_V1_0.md`
* **Strength:** The **Balanced Exemplar**. A massive document (~11.8k words) that expertly threaded high engagement, early round-table introductions, and sustained friction into highly actionable computational parameters.
