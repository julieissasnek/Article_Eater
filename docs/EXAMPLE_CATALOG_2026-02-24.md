## How to Use This Catalog

When building any section of the master paper, the Section Builder should:
1. Check this catalog for examples relevant to the section's domain.
2. Read the identified examples in their source files.
3. Incorporate the best examples into the section prose — either verbatim (if well-written) or rewritten (if the content is good but the prose needs work).
4. Cross-reference: if an example appears in multiple sources, use the most complete version.
5. Mark absorbed examples in this catalog with `[ABSORBED into §XX]`.

---

## Quality Tiers

- **Tier 1 — Exemplary**: Well-written, detailed, mechanistically complete, unique. Absorb with minimal rewriting.
- **Tier 2 — Strong**: Good content, clear structure, may need editorial polish or integration with surrounding prose.
- **Tier 3 — Useful**: Contains valuable data or illustrations but needs substantial rewriting or condensation.
