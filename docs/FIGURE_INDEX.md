## Design Standards

All figures follow the norms in `contracts/VISUALIZATION_NORMS.md`:
- Tufte data-ink ratio (maximize data, minimize decoration)
- Cleveland & McGill perceptual hierarchy
- Colorblind-safe palette (simulated with deuteranopia/protanopia checks)
- Direct labeling (no legends when possible)
- In-figure titles state findings, not topics
- Scientific American caption standard (4-8 sentences)
