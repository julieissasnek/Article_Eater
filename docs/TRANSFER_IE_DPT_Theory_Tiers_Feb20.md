# §3. IE-DPT: CURRENT STATUS

## 3.1 What IE-DPT Is

**Implicit-Explicit Dual-Process Theory** — a massive expansion and theoretical elevation of existing T1 #3 (DP / Dual-Process Evaluation). NOT a new 11th framework. Rather, it elevates DP to **superordinate status** over the other 9 T1 frameworks.

**Core claim**: The Goldilocks optimum for any environmental variable V is a function of both V and the activity frame A: G_opt(V) = f(V, A). Activity frame selection is a Type 2 (explicit) operation that configures boundary conditions for all Type 1 (implicit) processing. Without specifying the frame, predictions from any T1 theory are indeterminate.

# §5. EMERGENT STRUCTURAL FINDINGS

These cross-cutting observations emerged from the T1.5 expansion work and should inform all future development:

**Finding 1 — IE-DPT superordinate status confirmed empirically.** Every T1.5 reduction independently requires the explicit channel to explain its most consequential phenomenon: density ≠ crowding (Privacy Regulation), individual complexity optima (Kaplan Matrix), NV thermal adaptation (Adaptive Thermal Comfort). This is convergent evidence from three unrelated domains.

**Finding 2 — Two "super-templates" are emerging.** AX4 (Perceived Control) and IC2 (Body Budget Prediction) appear in nearly every T1.5 reduction. Control and predictability are universal modulators of environmental experience. May warrant formal elevation or special cross-reference status.

**Finding 3 — Irreducible residuals cluster in four categories.** (a) Explicit-channel effects → IE-DPT captures these. (b) Cultural/social norms → not yet specified in system. (c) Individual physiological variation → below cognitive level. (d) Temporal dynamics → partially covered by AX11 but may need extension.

**Finding 4 — Template re-use reveals hidden structure.** SC2 (Isovist), ENCLOSURE, T8 (Affordances), T66 (Learned Safety), AX4 (Control), IC2 (Body Budget) recur across phenomenologically unrelated domains. The template library is smaller than the theory library because many theories invoke the same mechanisms.

**Finding 5 — Nature/restoration bias corrected.** Original T1.5 roster: 4/4 theories in nature/restoration. Now: 7 reduced across nature, social-spatial, visual preference, and thermal domains. Still missing: acoustic, spatial configuration, place experience.
