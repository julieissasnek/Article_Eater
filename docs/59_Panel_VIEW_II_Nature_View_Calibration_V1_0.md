# PANEL VIEW-II: NATURE VIEW CALIBRATION
## Channel Weights, Synthetic Nature Efficacy, and View Quality Index for VIEW1
## February 17, 2026 — Document 59

---

## Panel Charge

Panel VIEW-I (Doc 47) produced VIEW1, a five-channel convergence model explaining how nature views affect wellbeing through fractal fluency (Ch1), prospect/spatial depth (Ch2), attention restoration (Ch3), temporal variation (Ch4), and ecological safety signaling (Ch5). VIEW-I identified three calibration gaps: no channel weights, no synthetic-nature hierarchy, and no operational view quality index. VIEW-II is charged with three deepening priorities:

**Deepening D1: Channel Separation and Weighting**. Assign relative weights to the five channels. Without weights, VIEW1 can classify but not quantify.

**Deepening D2: Synthetic Nature Efficacy Hierarchy**. Calibrate which delivery media (real view, courtyard, video, living wall, photograph, VR) engage which channels and at what fraction of the real-view benchmark.

**Deepening D3: View Quality Index (VQI)**. Develop an operational metric mapping observable view features to channel engagement and predicted wellbeing benefit.

## Position Statements

### D1: CHANNEL SEPARATION AND WEIGHTING

**PS-Hartig: The SRT-ART Dissociation Provides Channel Separation**

The most productive approach to separating Channel 3 (attention restoration) from Channel 5 (stress reduction) is to exploit their different temporal dynamics and different sensitivity to individual difference moderators (Hartig et al., 2003, ~2,000 citations; Hartig et al., 2014, ~3,000 citations).

Stress reduction (Channel 5) is FAST and AUTOMATIC. In our walking studies, cortisol and blood pressure begin declining within 5-10 minutes of nature exposure, regardless of the participant's initial attentional state. This response does not require that the participant be attention-depleted, and it occurs even during passive viewing. It is consistent with Ulrich's evolved safety-signal mechanism.

Attention restoration (Channel 3) is SLOWER and CONDITIONAL. Backward digit span and sustained-attention performance improve only after 15-20 minutes of nature exposure, and ONLY in participants who were previously attention-depleted. Well-rested participants show no attentional benefit from nature views. This conditionality is the signature of a restorative process.

This temporal and conditional dissociation means Channels 3 and 5 are EMPIRICALLY SEPARABLE. They operate through different mechanisms (parasympathetic activation vs. directed-attention recovery), on different timescales (minutes vs. tens of minutes), and with different boundary conditions (universal vs. depletion-dependent).

My estimated channel weights, based on explained variance in overall wellbeing outcomes:

- Channel 1 (Fractal fluency): 0.15
- Channel 2 (Prospect/depth): 0.20
- Channel 3 (Attention restoration): 0.25
- Channel 4 (Temporal variation): 0.10
- Channel 5 (Ecological safety): 0.30

**PS-Ulrich: Channel 5 Is Gating, Not Just Additive**

I broadly agree with Hartig's weights but want to emphasize something about the architecture of the model. Channel 5 (ecological safety) is not just the largest weight -- it is the ENABLING condition for the other channels. If the nature scene contains threat signals (dead vegetation, stagnant water, visible predator habitat), the ecological safety signal is NEGATIVE. The brain shifts from prediction-confirmation mode to threat-detection mode. Fractal fluency becomes irrelevant; soft fascination is overridden by hard vigilance.

This suggests Channel 5 is GATING. When Channel 5 is positive, the other channels contribute their benefits. When Channel 5 is negative or absent, the other channels are suppressed.

My proposed modification: VIEW_benefit = Ch5_gate x [w1 x Ch1 + w2 x Ch2 + w3 x Ch3 + w4 x Ch4] + w5 x Ch5_direct. Where Ch5_gate ranges from 0 (threat signals) through 1.0 (neutral) to 1.2 (strong positive safety signal).

**PS-Bratman: Neural Evidence Supports the Separation**

Our neuroimaging work (Bratman et al., 2015, ~2,500 citations) provides direct evidence for Channel 5's independence. Nature walks reduced self-reported rumination and decreased activity in the subgenual prefrontal cortex -- a region associated with repetitive negative self-referential thought. This is an AFFECTIVE regulation effect mediated by nature's impact on self-referential processing, neurally distinct from attention restoration.

My estimates, consistent with Hartig's: Channel 3 (cognitive restoration) ~25% of nature-view benefit, conditional on depletion. Channel 5 (affective/stress regulation) ~30%, unconditional.

**PS-Joye: Channel 1 May Be Underweighted**

My experimental work (Joye, 2007, ~400 citations; Joye & van den Berg, 2011, ~300 citations) shows that fractal patterns -- even ABSTRACT fractal patterns with no ecological content -- produce measurable stress reduction and preference effects. This suggests fractal fluency operates partly INDEPENDENTLY of ecological safety signaling. My estimate: Channel 1 should be 0.20, not 0.15.

**PS-Kaplan: Channel 3 Measurement Has Downward Bias**

Attention restoration is measured by attention TESTS which require effort and may themselves be depleting. The true restorative benefit may be underestimated. Our channel weights for Channel 3 may have downward bias.

### D2: SYNTHETIC NATURE EFFICACY HIERARCHY

**PS-Kahn: Technological Nature Is Better Than No Nature, But Worse Than Real**

Our plasma-display studies (Kahn et al., 2008, ~600 citations) provide the most direct test. Key findings:

- Heart rate recovery after stress: Real window > Plasma display > Blank wall
- Sustained attention: Real window approximate Plasma display > Blank wall (NO significant difference for attention)
- Preference and satisfaction: Real window >> Plasma display > Blank wall

The channel interpretation: the plasma display successfully engages Channel 1 (fractal statistics transmitted) and Channel 4 (temporal dynamics if real-time). It partially engages Channel 3. But it fails on Channel 2 (no depth) and partially fails on Channel 5 (brain detects it is not real).

**PS-White: Temporal Accumulation Modifies the Hierarchy**

Kahn's hierarchy is correct for ACUTE effects. But our 120-minute-per-week threshold data (White et al., 2019, ~1,500 citations) suggest that CUMULATIVE nature contact follows a different pattern. A living green wall visible 40 hours per week may produce better cumulative outcomes than a real window view accessible for brief breaks totaling 2 hours per week.

Cumulative benefit approximately equals Efficacy multiplied by Exposure_hours_per_week.

**PS-Gascon: Blue Space Engages Additional Channels**

Our epidemiological data (Gascon et al., 2015, ~500 citations; Gascon et al., 2017, ~400 citations) consistently find that blue-space proximity produces health benefits ADDITIVE with green-space benefits. Water views engage the standard five channels PLUS additional sub-channels: stronger temporal dynamics (waves, reflections) and a PRIMARY evolutionary resource signal (water access = survival). My estimate: a view including water adds approximately 15-25% to the overall VIEW1 benefit beyond equivalent green-only view.

### D3: VIEW QUALITY INDEX

**PS-Reinhart: The Index Must Be Computationally Derivable**

For the VQI to be practically useful, it must be computable from information available during architectural design. I can already compute view EXTENT using DIVA or Honeybee tools. What I cannot compute is view CONTENT.

**PS-Grahn: Nature Quality Has Measurable Dimensions**

My Perceived Sensory Dimensions (PSD) framework (Grahn & Stigsdotter, 2003, ~1,000 citations) identifies eight perceived qualities that map onto the five channels. The VQI could be operationalized as a weighted sum of PSD scores.

## Cross-Examination and Calibration Debates

### Debate 1: Is Channel 5 Gating or Additive?

**Ulrich**: Channel 5 gates the other channels. A threatening nature scene suppresses Channels 1-4.

**Hartig**: I see the logic but am cautious about multiplicative terms without direct experimental evidence. The degradation experiment from VIEW-I Prediction 1 would test this.

**Panel consensus**: **Present both models with preference for gated.** The additive model provides a lower bound. The gated model is more theoretically justified but requires new data.

### Debate 2: How Much Credit Does VR Nature Deserve?

**Kahn**: My data suggest VR/video at ~0.55-0.60 overall efficacy.

**Reinhart**: Modern VR has improved substantially since 2008. I would estimate 0.60-0.65.

**Joye**: VR fractal statistics are limited by display resolution. Channel 1 at 0.70-0.75.

**Panel consensus**: **VR nature at ~0.55-0.60 overall efficacy, a moving target** as technology improves. Key limitation is Channel 5 gating -- the brain partially discounts virtual environments.

**Convergence model (additive)**: VIEW_benefit = Sum(w_i x Ch_i_engagement)

**Convergence model (gated)**: VIEW_benefit = Ch5_gate x Sum(w_1-4 x Ch_1-4) + w_5 x Ch5_direct. Where Ch5_gate = max(0, min(1.2, 0.2 + Ch5_engagement)). Gate values: threat = 0.2 (suppressed); neutral = 0.7; strong positive = 1.2 (amplified).

**Super-additivity**: When all 5 channels engaged at high levels, gated model predicts ~15-20% super-additivity over additive model.

### Calibration Session 3: View Quality Index (VQI)

**Structure**: Score 0-100, computed from five sub-indices: VQI = 100 x Sum(w_i x Ch_i_score)

**Ch1 Score (Fractal Fluency, w = 0.18)**:
- Diverse natural canopy, mixed species: 1.00
- Managed park with mature trees: 0.80
- Manicured garden, regular planting: 0.50
- Scattered trees or hedges: 0.40
- Urban streetscape with some trees: 0.30
- Green wall / climbing plants: 0.25
- Built environment, no vegetation: 0.05
- Featureless surface: 0.00

**Ch2 Score (Prospect/Depth, w = 0.18)**:
- Panoramic landscape (> 500m): 1.00
- Open view, moderate depth (100-500m): 0.80
- Garden/courtyard (20-100m): 0.60
- Street view (20-50m): 0.50
- Near-field view (5-20m): 0.30
- Close obstruction (< 5m), partial sky: 0.15
- Complete obstruction (wall < 2m): 0.00

**Ch3 Score (Attention Restoration, w = 0.24)**:
- Rich biodiverse scene with water + wind: 1.00
- Leafy canopy with wind movement: 0.80
- Garden with some movement: 0.65
- Static vegetation, occasional movement: 0.40
- Minimal dynamic elements: 0.20
- No dynamic natural elements: 0.00

**Ch4 Score (Temporal Variation, w = 0.10)**:
- Water + sky + deciduous (full seasonal + diurnal): 1.00
- Sky + vegetation (seasonal + weather): 0.80
- Sky only (weather + light changes): 0.50
- Vegetation only (seasonal, limited diurnal): 0.40
- Built environment (minimal change): 0.10
- No exterior view: 0.00

**Ch5 Score (Ecological Safety, w = 0.30)**:
- Lush, well-maintained nature; water; no threat: 1.00
- Healthy green vegetation, no visible threat: 0.85
- Mixed nature/built, benign: 0.60
- Urban with scattered greenery: 0.45
- Built environment, neutral: 0.30
- Built with negative cues (industrial, degraded): 0.10
- Threat cues (dead vegetation, polluted): 0.00

**VQI Design Thresholds** (panel consensus, low confidence):
- VQI >= 70: Excellent -- full convergence, measurable health benefits
- VQI 50-69: Good -- substantial convergence, most channels active
- VQI 30-49: Adequate -- some channels active, partial benefits
- VQI 15-29: Poor -- minimal engagement, consider supplementation
- VQI < 15: Critical -- no meaningful benefit; synthetic nature recommended

**EN 17037 + VQI Integration**: EN 17037 assesses view EXTENT (window is large enough). VQI adds CONTENT assessment. Both should be reported.

## Panel Synthesis: What VIEW-II Accomplished

**For D1 (Channel Weights)**: Five channels weighted with Channel 5 (0.30) largest overall and Channel 3 (0.24) largest for cognitive outcomes. Outcome-specific profiles reveal that different outcomes prioritize different channels. Two convergence models: additive (conservative) and gated (with Channel 5 modulating). Super-additivity ~15-20%.

**For D2 (Synthetic Nature)**: Nine-level hierarchy from operable window (~1.05) through real window (1.00), courtyard (0.81-0.85), video/VR (0.47-0.54), living wall (0.36-0.37), photograph (0.20-0.24), potted plants (0.17-0.19), to no nature (0.00). Temporal accumulation can compensate for low acute efficacy. Blue-space bonus ~15-20%.

**For D3 (VQI)**: Five-channel scoring rubric. VQI 0-100 with design thresholds. EN 17037 integration. Computationally derivable for Ch1, Ch2, Ch4; requires field assessment for Ch3, Ch5.

## Testable Predictions

### Prediction 1: Channel Degradation (Gating vs. Additive)
Five conditions degrading one channel each. N = 120. If Ch5 degradation reduces total benefit by >45%, gated model supported.

### Prediction 2: Temporal Accumulation
4-week within-subjects: real window 30 min/day vs. living wall full day. N = 40. Prediction: living wall produces comparable weekly wellbeing.

### Prediction 3: VQI Validation
Compute VQI for 50 workstations in 5 buildings. N = 200 workers. Prediction: VQI predicts outcomes with r >= 0.40, outperforming EN 17037 extent-only.

### Prediction 4: Blue-Space Bonus
Green-only vs. green+water matched views. N = 60. Prediction: 15-25% greater stress recovery with water.

## Scope Exclusions

1. **Indoor air quality through operable windows**: Health benefits of ventilation are mechanistically distinct from view benefits.
2. **Therapeutic garden design**: Requires dedicated Healthcare Architecture panel.
3. **Soundscape as independent template**: Natural soundscapes noted as sub-channel; dedicated auditory-nature template justified but beyond scope.
