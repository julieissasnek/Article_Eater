# MODIFICATION 2: TWO-DOMAIN BRIDGE DEBATES (required)

The review's §3.1 concern about panel fragmentation is the most important
structural observation. Musical emotion researchers and architectural
acoustics researchers will talk past each other unless forced to engage.

**Mandate two cross-domain Crucible bridge debates** (in addition to
within-domain debates):

**Bridge Debate 1**: BRECVEMA_RHYTHMIC_ENTRAINMENT_002 × AUD_REVERBERATION_SPACE_003

The question: How does reverberation time modulate rhythmic entrainment?
Reverberation smears temporal envelopes. Short RT60 (~0.3-0.5s) preserves
beat clarity; long RT60 (>1.5s) degrades it (Grahn & Rowe, 2009 vs. Cox
room acoustics data). This debate forces Vuust (rhythm) and Cox
(reverberation) to negotiate a shared parameter space. The architectural
implication is direct: room acoustic design for spaces where rhythmic
sound matters (gyms, dance studios, worship spaces with congregational
singing).

**Bridge Debate 2**: ACOUSTIC_EMOTION_MAPPING_001 × MS_ACOUSTIC_ECOLOGY_001

The question: Are the acoustic features that drive musical emotion
(Juslin's cue utilization model) the same features that drive soundscape
appraisal (Kang's ISO 12913 descriptors)? Both use spectral centroid,
loudness variability, and temporal modulation as predictors — but the
outcome variables differ (discrete emotion vs. pleasantness/eventfulness).
This debate forces Juslin and Kang to determine whether there's a shared
acoustic→affect pathway or two parallel systems.

# MODIFICATION 4: PLEASURABLE_SADNESS_001 SCOPE DECISION (required)

The review raises the right question: the mechanism is well-studied but
the architectural bridge is narrow. Decision:

**Tier B treatment.** The mechanism (absorption, catharsis, aesthetic
distancing — Sachs et al., 2015) is well-established in music psychology
but the architectural application is confined to a small number of
settings (healthcare music therapy spaces, contemplative/memorial
architecture, performance venues). This limits the bridge warrant to
FUNCTIONAL at best (music induces the state; the architecture merely
provides the context). Tier B is appropriate — standard justification
depth, no competing-accounts section needed.
