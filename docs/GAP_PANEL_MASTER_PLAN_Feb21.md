# §1. SITUATION SUMMARY

The gap_registry_report confirms that 148 of 151 tracked templates are uncalibrated. Three have been calibrated by the STRESS-I panel (T6, T7, T14). The remaining 148 divide into 133 high-severity gaps (full parameterization required) and 15 medium-severity gaps (partial evidence exists; modeling work required). The scale of this task is substantial: even at 4–5 templates per panel, approximately 30–35 panel sessions are required to complete the registry.

The strategy adopted here is triaging by three criteria operating jointly: (1) architectural impact — does calibration of this template cluster materially change building design recommendations in high-stakes settings (healthcare, eldercare, education, workplace)? (2) Evidence density — is there a sufficient body of existing empirical literature to anchor the calibration, or would a panel be producing theoretical extrapolations with minimal empirical constraint? (3) Mechanistic centrality — do these templates provide parameters that feed into many other templates via interaction effects, such that calibrating them yields a cascade of downstream improvements?

The 12 panels are sequenced accordingly. Panels I–IV address the highest-impact, best-evidenced clusters. Panels V–VIII address mechanistically important but more speculative domains. Panels IX–XII handle the most theoretically peripheral or evidence-sparse clusters.
