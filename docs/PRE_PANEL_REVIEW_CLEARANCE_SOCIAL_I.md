## Roster changes (mandatory — apply before proceeding)

### Change 1: Replace Dunbar as primary on CROSS_SOCIAL_MIRROR_PRESENCE_001

Robin Dunbar's social brain hypothesis addresses evolutionary group-size constraints,
not embodied social presence or mirror mechanisms. The template requires the
neuroscience of co-presence — what happens in the motor-simulation and social-
engagement systems when people share architectural space.

**Add Vittorio Gallese** (University of Parma) as primary anchor for
CROSS_SOCIAL_MIRROR_PRESENCE_001. Gallese originated the shared manifold hypothesis
and has written explicitly on embodied simulation in architectural experience
(Gallese & Gattara, 2015). His simulation theory provides the mechanism chain:
architectural configuration → visual/motor access to other agents → mirror system
activation → felt co-presence.

**Add Leonhard Schilbach** (LMU Munich) as complement on
CROSS_SOCIAL_MIRROR_PRESENCE_001 and as primary anchor on
CROSS_SOCIAL_AFFORDANCE_READING_001. Schilbach's second-person neuroscience
distinguishes observing someone from being engaged with someone in shared space —
precisely the distinction the co-presence template must calibrate (passive social
observation vs. active social engagement). His work also directly addresses social
affordance reading in interactive contexts, giving CROSS_SOCIAL_AFFORDANCE_READING_001
a named primary anchor it previously lacked.

**Reassign Robin Dunbar** to complement on XF_SOCIAL_AFFORDANCE_DENSITY_001
alongside Al-Sayed. Social affordance density — how many people can productively
share a space — is where evolutionary group-size constraints actually contribute.
Dunbar's number and the scaling data are relevant inputs to the density template's
upper-bound parameters.

### Change 2: Confirm Gifford double assignment

Robert Gifford covers both PRIVACY_GRADIENT_REGULATION_001 and
TERRITORIAL_AFFORDANCE_SOCIAL_001. Both derive from Altman's (1975) framework.
The territorial affordance template had no named primary anchor in the original
roster. Gifford's environmental psychology of privacy, crowding, and territorial
behavior makes this a natural double assignment, not a stretch.

## Constraints to enforce during calibration and post-panel review

1. **Porges constraint**: Confidence scores for parameters derived from the
   phylogenetic autonomic hierarchy (dorsal vagal, ventral vagal, sympathetic
   ordering) must carry THEORETICAL_DEFAULT. Only cardiac vagal tone measures
   (RMSSD, RSA) and the social engagement system behavioral markers have
   sufficient empirical support for confidence > 0.50.

2. **Young and Saxe translation ceiling**: Both are molecular/cognitive
   neuroscientists whose work does not directly address architectural variables.
   Bridge warrants from their calibration constructs to architectural outcome
   measures must not exceed EMPIRICAL_COVARIANCE. MECHANISM warrant requires
   a traced and tested pathway in the architectural context, which neither
   provides.

3. **VIEW1 Channel 5 double-counting**: NM_VAGAL_REGULATION_001's amygdala-
   reduction mechanism overlaps with VIEW1 Channel 5 (ecological safety via
   amygdala). The panel must encode a partial-out or ceiling on combined
   contribution. Flag for CROSS_TEMPLATE_INTERACTION → NEUROMOD-I if not
   resolved within SOCIAL-I.
