## Constraints to enforce during calibration and post-panel review

1. **CT afferent body-site translation**: Morrison's data are from forearm
   stroking at ~3 cm/s. Architectural touch (hand on railing, foot on floor,
   back against chair) involves different body sites, contact pressures, and
   velocities. Bridge warrant for architectural surface parameters: CAPACITY
   (0.45) maximum. Not MECHANISM — the pathway is established for forearm
   but not for architectural contact modes.

2. **Ernst material integration — bridge ceiling**: Bayesian causal inference
   model is computationally strong but tested with simple laboratory stimuli.
   Translation to architectural materials (complex, multi-feature, embedded
   in spatial context) is untested. Bridge warrant for architectural
   parameters: FUNCTIONAL (0.50) maximum.

3. **Inverse effectiveness neural mechanism**: Stein's data are from cat
   superior colliculus. Human behavioral analogues exist. Architectural
   translation (dim lighting enhances haptic/auditory impact): behavioral
   effect at EMPIRICAL_COVARIANCE, neural mechanism at THEORETICAL_DEFAULT.

4. **Material cultural conditioning — population specificity**: All parameters
   are population-dependent. Marble, concrete, wood, steel carry different
   evaluative associations across cultures. The template must specify which
   population the parameters apply to. No universal cultural conditioning
   parameters — flag any as error.

5. **Pallasmaa parameters — empirical ceiling**: Any parameter anchored
   solely by Pallasmaa's phenomenological claims without independent
   empirical support: confidence ≤ 0.40, mandatory THEORETICAL_DEFAULT.
   Mikellides as complement provides the empirical check.

6. **VISUAL-I partial-out**: VISUAL-I T1 owns visual fractal/texture
   statistics. MULTI-I owns the multi-channel convergence bonus that accrues
   when visual statistics are accompanied by congruent haptic and olfactory
   natural material signals. The builder AI computes: visual channel effect
   (from VISUAL-I T1) + convergence bonus (from MULTI-I
   NATURAL_MATERIAL_CONVERGENCE_001). Not: visual channel from both.
