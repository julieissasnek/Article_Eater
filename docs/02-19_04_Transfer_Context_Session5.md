## KEY THEORETICAL POSITIONS DEVELOPED

### Goldilocks Principle
- **Multi-channel independence**: Inverted-U operates independently per sensory channel (visual complexity, acoustics, temperature, wood coverage, fractal D, ceiling height, light)
- **Cross-modal compensation**: Channels interact through shared LC-NE arousal pathway. Quiet room needs more visual complexity; noisy room needs less
- **Task dependence**: Creative tasks prefer higher PE than focused tasks. Same room can be Goldilocks-optimal for brainstorming and anti-optimal for code review
- **Cultural parameterization**: India fieldwork shows correlated set-point elevation across ALL modalities simultaneously → suggests single underlying parameter, not independent prior calibration
- **PP relationship**: PP explains WHY inverted-U exists (zero PE = boredom, moderate = engagement, excessive = anxiety). But PP has 5 limitations:
  1. Normative gap (dark room problem — PP says minimize, Goldilocks says optimize)
  2. Task-specific optima (PP doesn't predict creative/focused difference)
  3. Multi-channel structure (PP operates within modalities, doesn't predict cross-modal compensation)
  4. Cultural set-points (PP predicts modality-specific shifts; India shows correlated shift)
  5. Prospect-refuge (PP predicts monotonic max prospect + max refuge; Goldilocks observes inverted-U — Dosen & Ostwald 2016)

### Hedonic Tagging — The Second-Order PE Account
- **Core claim**: Valence = ∂PE/∂t. PE high and decreasing = positive (model improving). PE high and increasing = negative (model failing). PE low and stable = boredom. PE moderate and stable = flow/Goldilocks.
- **Key proponents**: Joffily & Coricelli (2013), Van de Cruys (2017), Hesp et al. (2021)
- **Neural implementation**: Dopamine tags successful PE reduction as rewarding (Bromberg-Martin & Hikosaka, 2009). Anterior insula/dACC tags unresolved PE as aversive. OFC convergence point where hedonic tags bind to perceptual representations.
- **Connection to second-order learning**: HGF models (Mathys et al., 2011, 2014). Brain tracks volatility (how fast world changes) and meta-volatility. Goldilocks zone = well-calibrated volatility estimate.

### Unresolved Issues with Hedonic Tagging
Six problems identified:
1. **Empirical thinness**: Joffily/Coricelli/Hesp work is computational, not empirical. No experiment cleanly dissociates first-order PE magnitude from second-order trajectory
2. **Timescale problem**: Hedonic responses in 300-500ms; second-order stats need a temporal window. Zajonc (1980) "preferences need no inferences"
3. **Bootstrapping problem**: Why is model improvement rewarding? Evolutionary argument ≠ mechanistic explanation
4. **Basic-emotions challenge**: Neonatal hedonic responses (sweet = pleasure, bitter = disgust within hours of birth) don't require second-order computation. Some hedonic tags are innate.
5. **Individual differences**: Neuroticism, SPS, intolerance of uncertainty modulate how same PE is experienced
6. **Measurement problem**: No neural measure unambiguously reads out ∂PE/∂t as distinct from current PE magnitude or arousal

**Best supporting evidence** (indirect): Curiosity research (Bromberg-Martin & Hikosaka, 2009; Kang et al., 2009; Gruber et al., 2014; Kidd & Hayden, 2015), aesthetic fluency (Vessel et al., 2012; Van de Cruys & Wagemans, 2011).

### Andy Clark's Position on PP and Affect

**General PP framework:**
- Brain as hierarchical prediction engine. Three operations: perceptual inference, active inference, attention (precision-weighting)
- **Radical Predictive Processing** (vs. Conservative): fast, frugal, action-involving, world-leaning. Not neo-Cartesian inner theater. Connects to extended mind thesis (Clark & Chalmers, 1998)
- This makes Clark's PP most compatible with neuroarchitecture: if prediction extends into environment, architecture IS cognitive infrastructure

**On emotion/affect specifically:**
- Key papers: Miller & Clark (2018) "Happily Entangled"; Clark (2018) "A Nice Surprise?"; Wilkinson, Deane, Nave & Clark (2019) "Getting Warmer"
- **Emotion is NOT separate from prediction** — constitutively entangled through continuous reciprocal causation between cortical and subcortical systems. Pulvinar as gain-control mechanism
- **Interoceptive prediction is central**: Environmental hedonic quality partly = interoceptive PE (predicted vs. actual bodily states). Claustrophobic room predicts elevated heart rate; prospect-refuge predicts relaxation
- **Dark room solution**: Rejects single information-theoretic quantity. Requires three things: (1) evolved prestructuring (homeostatic priors), (2) epistemic foraging (expected free energy), (3) cultural designer environments. Cites infant Goldilocks effect (Kidd et al., 2012)
- **Key difference from Joffily/Hesp**: Clark does NOT endorse valence = ∂PE/∂t. His position: hedonic quality is emergent from whole-system dynamics (brain + body + environment), not reducible to a single computational quantity. More realistic but less tractable.

**What Clark's view supports for David's framework:**
- Environment as cognitive scaffold for prediction: architecture IS part of the extended predictive system
- Interoceptive channel as design variable (temperature, posture, proprioception, olfaction directly affect hedonic experience through interoceptive PE)
- Active sampling matters: building's hedonic impact depends on how it supports/constrains epistemic action (affordances for exploration, vantage points, progressive revelation)
- Cultural niche construction shapes precision landscape → Varanasi/Stockholm comparison directly follows

## EXTRACTION 1: CHILD DEVELOPMENT GOLDILOCKS LITERATURE

### Core Finding (Kidd, Piantadosi, & Aslin, 2012 [GS: ~700])
- 7-8 month old infants: U-shaped looking-away probability as function of event complexity (negative log probability from ideal Bayesian learner)
- Infants terminate attention to events that are either too predictable (nothing to learn) or too surprising (unlearnable)
- Maximum sustained attention at **intermediate complexity (~1.25 bits)**
- Complexity computed *relative to infant's inferred knowledge state* — Goldilocks zone is a moving target, not fixed

### Extensions
- **Auditory modality**: Same U-shape for sound sequences (Kidd et al., 2014, *Child Development* [GS: ~200]) → cross-modal, domain-general
- **Within-individual**: Pattern holds within each infant, not just across population (Piantadosi, Kidd, & Aslin, 2014 [GS: ~150])
- **Cross-species**: Rhesus monkeys show same pattern spontaneously (Wu, Blanchard, Meschke, Aslin, Hayden, & Kidd) → phylogenetically deep, not cultural
- **Curiosity as explore/exploit**: Kidd & Hayden (2015 [GS: ~700], *Neuron*) — Goldilocks zone optimizes information gain per unit cognitive investment

### Relevance to David's Goldilocks Elaboration
1. Grounds inverted-U not in adult aesthetic taste but in **fundamental primate attentional-learning architecture** from 7 months
2. Cross-modal evidence directly parallels 7-channel framework
3. Knowledge-relative complexity = PP's prior-relative PE. Same mechanism as cultural set-point shifts
4. Developmental trajectory: Goldilocks zone shifts upward with learning = ontogenetic analog of cultural set-point elevation (Varanasi)
5. Explicitly connects to explore/exploit tradeoff in curiosity literature

## EXTRACTION 2: CLARK → CNfA MAPPING

### A. Environment as Unconscious Explore/Exploit Regulator
- Clark (2018) "A Nice Surprise?": PP brain engages **epistemic foraging** — seeks states that reduce long-term uncertainty
- Built environment = foraging landscape for epistemic action
- Precision-weighting (set by pulvinar, LC-NE, basal ganglia DA) determines explore vs. exploit mode
- Environment modulates precision directly, below conscious threshold
- Novel moderate-complexity space → phasic LC → explore. Familiar predicted space → tonic LC → exploit
- **Design principle**: Architecture sets the explore/exploit dial unconsciously

### B. Environment as Interoceptive Prediction Surface
- Miller & Clark (2018): PP integrates exteroceptive + interoceptive predictions simultaneously
- Entering room = predicting visual/auditory scene AND body state (heart rate, muscle tension, thermal, respiratory)
- Temperature, lighting CCT, air quality, posture, olfaction are **first-class channels in generative model**, not secondary comfort
- Interoceptive PE contributes to hedonic tag equally with exteroceptive PE
- Bad furniture → chronic proprioceptive PE → precision drained from task channel
- **Design principle**: Every non-task PE channel competes for precision with the task

### C. Active Inference and Architectural Affordances
- Organisms reduce PE through action (changing world to match prediction), not just perception (updating model)
- Affordances in PP terms = **expected PE-reduction trajectories**
- Operable windows, adjustable lighting, moveable furniture, multiple zones = rich active inference degrees of freedom
- Sealed/fixed/uniform room = active inference blocked → all PE reduction must be internal → metabolically expensive
- **Design principle**: Good architecture supports active inference; bad architecture forces only perceptual inference

### D. Environmental Scaffolding = Precision Management
- Clark's extended mind (1998) + PP: environment is part of generative model
- Well-organized workspace = external predictions, offloaded from cortical processing
- Stable lighting (500 lux, 4000K) → visual PE ≈ 0 → all precision to task content
- Stable temperature (22°C) → thermal PE ≈ 0 → precision freed for cognition
- Good ergonomics → proprioceptive PE ≈ 0 → precision freed for task
- **Total task-available precision = total budget − precision allocated to non-task PE channels**
- **Strongest Clark-derived claim: ARCHITECTURE IS PRECISION MANAGEMENT**
- Architect's job in PP terms: drive non-task PE channels to zero (or Goldilocks optimum for beneficial channels), maximizing precision available for occupant's goals

### Key Theoretical Points to Preserve from This Session's Discussion:

**The depth-of-processing argument**: Same stimulus can be processed at different depths depending on active conjectures. Depth = how high in predictive hierarchy PE propagates before resolution. Craik & Lockhart (1972) reframed: shallow processing = PE resolved at low cortical levels; deep processing = PE propagates to PFC requiring model revision, theory comparison, autobiographical integration.

**The multiple-conjecture problem**: Expert cognition involves multiple simultaneous inference streams at different hierarchical levels (sequence prediction, model selection, ecological inference, cross-domain theory building). These levels have different optimal PE profiles and can conflict about what's maximally informative.

**The homunculus problem for PP**: PP eliminates homunculus for automatic processing (self-organizing system). But for deliberate cognition — consciously choosing which conjecture to pursue — PP either (a) smuggles homunculus back as "high-level priors" or (b) denies deliberate choice is real. Neither is satisfying.

**The two-regime architecture**:
- Implicit regime: Goldilocks-sensitive, PE-magnitude-driven, info-rate is critical variable
- Explicit regime: interruption-sensitive, PE-variance-driven, uninterrupted duration is critical variable
- Best environments support BOTH simultaneously

**The mean vs. variance principle**: Implicit processing sensitive to PE distribution mean; explicit processing sensitive to PE distribution variance. Room at 45 dB with 70 dB spikes worse than room at 50 dB steady for deep work. Explicit processing optimizes on precision-of-PE-signal (how predictable the prediction errors are).

**Architecture is precision management (extended)**: For implicit regime, architect drives non-task PE channels to Goldilocks optimum. For explicit regime, architect minimizes PE variance over time to protect deliberative workspace. Both operate simultaneously.
