## Proposed calibration constraints (for reviewer consideration)

1. **CT afferent firing parameters**: Morrison's data are from controlled stroking paradigms (forearm, optimal velocity ~3 cm/s). Translation to architectural surface touch (hand on railing, foot on floor, back against chair) requires CAPACITY warrant at best — the body sites, contact pressures, and exploration velocities differ from the lab paradigm.

2. **Inverse effectiveness**: Stein's data are primarily from cat superior colliculus neurons. Human behavioral analogues exist (Rowland & Stein, 2014) but the neural mechanism is extrapolated. Architectural translation (e.g., dim lighting enhances the impact of auditory and haptic cues) should be EMPIRICAL_COVARIANCE for the behavioral effect, THEORETICAL_DEFAULT for the neural mechanism.

3. **Material cultural conditioning**: This is inherently population-specific. Marble connotes luxury in Western/Middle Eastern contexts but may connote coldness or sterility in Nordic contexts. The template must specify that cultural conditioning parameters are population-dependent and not universal.

4. **Patina / temporal depth**: Pallasmaa's claims are philosophical. Empirical support for patina enhancing architectural experience is sparse. Confidence scores should be conservative (≤ 0.40) unless Mikellides or other empiricists provide supporting data.
