## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. All Candidates Fall Short by 56-59 Percentage Points
No candidate reaches even 10% of required template coverage. The gap is too large for incremental development.

### 2. All Four Theories Are Already Explained by T1 Frameworks
- **Episodic Memory** → explained by MS (Memory Systems) and SN (Spatial Navigation)
- **Berlyne's Arousal** → absorbed into PP (Goldilocks principle) and NM (neuromodulation)
- **Predictive Coding of Music** → music is one domain where PP operates
- **Auditory Scene Analysis** → MSI (Multisensory Integration) and PP explain most mechanisms

**Conclusion**: These are valuable Tier 2 phenomenological schemas, not mechanistically novel Tier 1.5 frameworks.

### 3. Auditory Scene Analysis Is Strongest but Still Inadequate
- Only candidate with mechanistically irreducible claims (stream segregation capacity limits)
- Auditory domain is genuinely underdeveloped (5-7% of templates vs. 24% for visual)
- Realistic development could add 15-20 auditory templates, reaching ~10% coverage
- **Even then**: Would need auditory domain to become 60% of total system for ASA to reach 60% threshold
- **Verdict**: Conditional deferral pending auditory domain expansion

### 4. System Architecture Is Mature, Not Incomplete
The framework is not missing core theories. Rather, it has:
- **8 complete T1 frameworks** (PP, SN, DT, DM, NM, IC, MS, MSI, etc.)
- **Uneven domain coverage** (visual 24%, auditory 5-7%, material 4-5%)
- **Clear Tier 2 organization** (phenomenological schemas like Berlyne, episodic memory, etc.)

**Strategic implication**: Future development should focus on **domain expansion**, not theory promotion.

## RECOMMENDATION FOR EACH CANDIDATE

### Episodic Memory Theory → KEEP IN TIER 2

**Action**: Deepen MS framework to mechanistically explain episodic specificity
**Timeline**: Optional; medium priority

### Berlyne's Arousal Theory → KEEP IN TIER 2
**Status**: Already integrated into PP (Goldilocks) and NM frameworks; remains Tier 2
**Action**: Develop reductionist account showing how PP/NM explain Berlyne phenomena
**Timeline**: Medium priority for theoretical completeness

### Predictive Coding of Music → KEEP IN TIER 2
**Status**: Music is one domain where PP operates; remains Tier 2
**Action**: Develop 5-8 music-specific auditory templates within PP framework
**Timeline**: Low priority; music is peripheral to core neuroarchitecture focus

### Auditory Scene Analysis → DEFER (Conditional)
**Status**: Mechanistically justified; strongest candidate; remains Tier 2 pending development
**Action**: Invest in systematic auditory domain expansion (15-20 new templates)
**Milestones**:
- Sprint N: Add 5 templates on speech intelligibility and acoustic masking
- Sprint N+1: Add 5 templates on reverberation and stream coherence in spaces
- Sprint N+2: Add 5-10 templates on acoustic stress pathways and privacy effects
- **Re-assessment**: At 15+ total auditory templates (8-10% coverage), re-evaluate whether to pursue full T1.5 qualification

**Re-evaluation criteria**:
- Does ASA explain phenomena that MSI/PP don't? (Stream segregation capacity limits: YES, at least partially)
- Is there irreducible mechanistic content? (Yes, but may be subsumable under MSI/working memory constraints)
- Does system benefit from ASA as organizing principle? (Depends on whether auditory becomes design focus)

## WHAT THIS MEANS

### For the System Architecture
✓ **Good news**: T1 frameworks are robust; no critical missing theories
✗ **Action needed**: Auditory domain is underdeveloped relative to visual and spatial domains

### For Future T1.5 Candidates
**Don't promote existing theories.** Instead:
1. **Develop new domains** until they reach 10-15% of template base
2. **Check for mechanistically irreducible claims** (what does this theory explain that T1 doesn't?)
3. **Evaluate whether separate T1.5 status adds value** (is fragmentation worth the organized focus?)
4. **Only pursue full T1.5 if domain becomes 60%+ of system** (unrealistic for niches) OR **if theory has mechanistically novel vocabulary** (like ASA stream segregation limits)

### For Rejected Candidates
- Keep in Tier 2 as phenomenological schemas
- Document how T1 frameworks mechanistically explain their claims
- Use as validation tests for T1 framework predictions
- Don't pursue T1.5 status
