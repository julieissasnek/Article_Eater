# Synthesizer Workflow (Human-in-the-Loop)

The Synthesizer tab is where you turn vetted findings into candidate rules.

**Core responsibilities**

- Group related findings into higher-level mechanisms or constructs.
- Resolve contradictions or overlaps flagged by the Aggregator.
- Approve or revise rule candidates before they are exported to the BN layer.

The detailed, student-facing UI copy for this flow is maintained in
`GUI_Verbiage_Package.docx`.
