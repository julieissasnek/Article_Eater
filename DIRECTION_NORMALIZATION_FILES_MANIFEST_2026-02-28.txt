QUESTIONS & SUPPORT

Q: How do I verify the normalization worked?
A: Open DIRECTION_FIELD_NORMALIZATION_REPORT_2026-02-28.md and review
   the "Verification Results" section. All 32,819 findings are confirmed
   to have canonical direction values.

Q: Can I undo the changes?
A: Yes. The entire original dataset is backed up in:
   data/extractions_backup_2026-02-28/
   Run: cp -r data/extractions_backup_2026-02-28/* data/extractions/

Q: What are the 4 canonical values?
A: 1. increase (positive/enhancing effects)
   2. decrease (negative/reducing effects)
   3. no_effect (null/non-significant effects)
   4. mixed (complex/conditional/bidirectional effects)

Q: What are "claim type leaks"?
A: 1,789 findings where values like "causal", "associational", or "modulates"
   (which should be in the claim_type field) were instead in the direction field.
   These are now flagged and mapped to "mixed" for expert review.

Q: What does "unmapped" mean?
A: 1,795 findings had direction values that couldn't be reliably mapped to
   a single canonical category (e.g., "inherent", "present", "elicit").
   These are conservatively defaulted to "mixed" for later expert validation.

Q: How do I regenerate the normalization?
A: Run: python3 normalize_direction_field.py
   The script will repeat the entire process using the same mapping rules.

Q: Where's the mapping table?
A: In data/direction_normalization_map.json
   This JSON file contains all 1,723 original → canonical mappings with counts.
