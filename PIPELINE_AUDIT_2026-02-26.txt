3.3 WHAT GETS EXTRACTED
-----------------------

Per article type (from gemini_extraction_queue.py):

EMPIRICAL STUDIES:
  - findings[]: antecedent, consequent, direction, claim_type, measure_type, p_value, effect_size, 
               confidence_interval, sample_size, theory_links, mechanism, source (Table/text), quote
  - study_design: experiment, survey, field_study, observational, case_study, mixed
  - n_participants: total sample size
  - stimuli[]: type, description, source
  - domains: CNFA domains affected (A1_Materials, A2_Spatial_Scale, A4_Light, etc.)
  - overall_theory_links: frameworks guiding hypotheses

META-ANALYSIS:
  - pooled_effects[]: effect_size, CI, heterogeneity (I²), k (number of studies), N (total participants)
  - comparison: comparison being made
  - publication_bias_assessment
  - study_list: component studies included

SYSTEMATIC REVIEW:
  - included_studies[]: doi, title, year, n_participants, study_design, outcomes
  - selection_process: PRISMA compliance, inclusion/exclusion criteria
  - quality_assessment: risk of bias for key studies

NARRATIVE REVIEW:
  - key_claims[]: claim text, support_strength, references
  - research_gaps: identified gaps
  - methodological_issues: quality assessment

THEORETICAL:
  - propositions[]: proposition text, logical form, supporting constructs
  - mechanisms: causal pathways proposed
  - scope_conditions: when/where the theory applies
  - falsifiability_assessment

All results tagged with:
  - confidence (0.0-1.0)
  - source (page number or table/figure reference)
  - theory_links (which of 10 T1 + 5 domain theories apply)
