CRITICAL ISSUE #1: DUPLICATE INSTRUMENT ABBREVIATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Severity: CRITICAL
Scope: instruments_registry.json

Finding:
  The abbreviation "VAS" appears TWICE in the registry:
  - VAS (instrument_id: 'VAS')
  - VAS-PAIN (instrument_id: 'VAS-PAIN')

  This violates the assumption that abbreviation is a unique key.

Impact:
  When outcome_vocab terms reference "VAS", it is ambiguous which instrument
  is meant. Reverse lookup breaks.

Expected:
  All abbreviations must be globally unique. Either:
  1. Rename VAS-PAIN to something else (e.g., "VASPAIN", "VAS-P")
  2. Use instrument_id as the primary reference key in outcome_vocab
     (currently uses abbreviation)

Risk: MEDIUM - Affects vocabulary→instrument mapping resolution

Findings:
  - outcome_vocab.json: 116 terms defined
  - outcome_lookup.json: 5 entries (COMPLETELY OUT OF SYNC)

  Missing from lookup (116 expected, 5 present):
    All 116 vocabulary terms should have entries in outcome_lookup.json
    Currently only 5 entries present (likely stale/partial)

  Extra entries in lookup (not in vocab):
    5 terms in outcome_lookup not found in vocabulary

Impact:
  outcome_lookup is the fast-access index for term resolution. It is
  completely out of sync with the canonical vocabulary. This means:
  1. Fast lookups will miss 95% of terms

  3. Inconsistent results if some code uses vocab and others use lookup

Expected:
  outcome_lookup.json must be regenerated to match outcome_vocab.json
  exactly. Every term in outcome_vocab should appear in outcome_lookup.

Risk: CRITICAL - Breaks term resolution and caching
