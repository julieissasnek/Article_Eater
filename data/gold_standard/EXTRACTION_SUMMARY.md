## Core Mechanism Identified

**Alert Relaxation Framework**: The wooden interior environment creates an optimal psychophysiological state characterized by:
- High alpha-to-theta ratio (balanced internal/external attention)
- High alpha-to-beta ratio (relaxed state with reduced strain)
- Low theta-to-beta ratio (maintained focus with reduced cognitive load)
- High self-reported relaxation and positive affect
- Superior cognitive task performance

This multimodal state optimizes task execution through simultaneous emotional regulation, neural balance, and environmental support.

## Limitations Acknowledged

1. Sample limited to Korean college students (generalizability concern)
2. VR-based stimuli may not capture full multisensory reality (tactile, thermal, olfactory)
3. Wooden interior condition maximized wood exposure (potentially overrepresenting real-world effects)
4. Natural element proportions not systematically quantified across conditions
5. Single space design limits generalization across diverse spatial typologies
6. Study exploratory; findings require replication and panel review
