🚀 Getting Started with Article Eater

1. 🔍 Start a Search

Use the New Search page to submit queries to Semantic Scholar. Specify your research topic and let the system find relevant papers.

2. 📚 Build Your Library

Review discovered papers in your Library. Filter, sort, and select the most relevant research for your synthesis.

3. 🔬 Extract Findings

Let AI extract structured findings from papers, including statistics, effect sizes, and evidence quality indicators.

4. 📝 Review Rules

Examine synthesized rules in the Rule Inspector. Each rule combines evidence from multiple papers with confidence scores.
