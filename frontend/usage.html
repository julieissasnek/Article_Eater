💡 Cost-Saving Tips

Use L1 Triage: Filter papers before expensive L2 extraction. This can save 60-80% of costs.
Batch Queries: Group similar searches together to reuse embeddings and reduce API calls.
Enable Caching: Already processed papers won't be re-extracted, saving money.
Set Budget Alerts: Get notified at 75% and 90% of your budget to avoid overruns.
